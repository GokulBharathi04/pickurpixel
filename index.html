Before you ask

The questions we hear most. Anything else, call or WhatsApp us.

How long does a website take?

Most small business websites go live in 2–4 weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content and feedback come in.

Do I need to write the content myself?

No. Share the basics about your business and we'll help shape the text. Photos of your work make the biggest difference.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops before launch.

Will I show up on Google?

We set up SEO, Search Console and your Google Business Profile so Google can find and list you. Rankings build over time, and local searches usually respond fastest.

Can I update the website later?

Yes. Small changes can be handled under a Care Plan, or we can quote for larger updates as you grow.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

Yes. We can register your domain and set up hosting, or work with what you already have.

How does payment work?

50% in advance when you confirm the project, and the remaining 50% on launch.

Can you build billing software just for my business?

Yes. We build it around your workflow (your invoice format, your customers, your reports) rather than selling you a one-size-fits-all tool. It's web-based, so it works from any browser, and we train your team and support you after go-live.
